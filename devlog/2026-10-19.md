# Day 1 · 19 Oct 2026 · Ground truth first

**TL;DR:** Before changing any code, we built a labelled ground truth from our own Claude Code sessions. Of 357 check claims ("tests pass", "build passes", "lint is clean"), half were backed by a real run, about 1 in 5 had no proof at all, and outright contradictions were rare. We also found that our own default install misses these at the end of a turn. Fixing that is v0.3's first job.

## The Ground Truth Test (GTT-1)

**Data:** 640 candidate sentences, a stratified sample from 150 of our own Claude Code sessions. They are split by session lineage into a working half (DEV, 310 rows) and a sealed half (TEST, 320 rows); 10 rows that would have crossed the split are kept out of both.

**Labels:**
- One grader labelled every row against a written rulebook (codebook).
- A second grader re-labelled a 120-row overlap blind. Both graders are AI models.
- Disagreements were settled by a third, stronger model, and logged row by row.
- Truth comes from the record, never from the agent's words. For 118 rows it also comes from an independent oracle: gate logs or re-runs.

**Agreement (Cohen's kappa, 120 overlap rows, before any rulebook change):**

| Question | kappa |
| --- | --- |
| Is this sentence a check claim? | 0.966 |
| Verdict (6 classes) | 0.816 |
| Verdict and reason | 0.686 |

Verdict and reason fell under our 0.70 bar, so the rulebook was revised once and the affected rows relabelled. After the revision these read 0.925 to 0.953, but those figures flatter us, because the new rules were written from the same disagreements. The table above holds the numbers we stand behind.

**What the record showed when the agent made the claim (357 check claims in the labelled sample):**

| Verdict | Count | Share | 95% range (Wilson) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Backed: the check had run and passed | 178 | 50% | 45–55% |
| No proof at all | 80 | 22% | 18–27% |
| Couldn't be checked from the record | 92 | 26% | 22–31% |
| Contradicted by the record | 5 | 1.4% | 0.6–3.2% |

**Notes:**
- The sample is stratified, so these are shares of the labelled sample, not a census of every claim.
- The TEST half's labels were hashed (sha256) before any code change. TEST is opened once, for the final run.
- With 5 contradicted claims in total, we cannot validate blocking on "contradicted" (we require 30 or more). ThruLine's end-of-turn check stays non-blocking on that verdict.

**What it means:** agents rarely state the opposite of what happened. The real gap is the 1 in 5 claims with nothing behind them, and the 1 in 4 that nobody can verify from the record.

## What we found about our own default install

In a controlled test of 24 built cases, 9 with a planted false claim, ThruLine v0.2 installed exactly as our README says flagged **0 of the 9** at the end of the turn. The reason: the default install doesn't record test runs. That needs `thruline shim install`, which the quick start skips. That's why v0.3 starts with run capture.
